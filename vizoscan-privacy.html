VizoScan AI

PREMIUM DOCUMENT SUITE

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

At VizoScan AI, we believe your private documents should stay private. Our "Golden Rule" is absolute: Your document data never leaves your device. We run a fully decentralized, offline-first application ecosystem.

1. 100% On-Device Processing & Editing

VizoScan AI uses advanced machine learning kits and local text modifying engines to process your data. All text recognition (OCR), image perspective cropping, document parsing, and file modifications happen entirely offline on your local hardware. We do not maintain or communicate with cloud servers to process, edit, or store your documents.

2. Local Workspace & Recycle Bin Architecture

To help you manage files safely, the app includes a local file system layer:

  • Text Modification Engine: Modifying .txt elements is handled directly inside the app's secure runtime memory buffers and written straight back to your local file.
  • Recycle Bin Workspace: When you delete an asset, it is safely moved to a hidden, isolated .trash directory within your device's app sandbox storage. It remains there until you manually restore it or select a permanent wipe.

3. Modern Permissions & Security Features

  • Camera Framework: Used only when you initialize an active image capture loop or a document scan. Frames are held momentarily in device cache to handle perspective cropping and are never distributed outside the app.
  • System Photo Picker: We leverage the secure, native Android Photo Picker. This guarantees that our system can only see the specific images you explicitly tap and select.
  • Scoped App Storage: Documents are generated and kept securely inside isolated application sandboxed storage slots. This prevents other malicious apps installed on your device from tracking or accessing your data files.
  • Home Screen Widgets: Our quick-shortcut app widgets run entirely on local intent receivers to trigger instant scans or hop to your local directory lists without collecting data.

4. Data Safety Compliance

To maintain app stability, we may track completely anonymized diagnostic analytics (such as Firebase Crashlytics). This technical data covers basic system crashes and app feature usage metrics, but it never includes, logs, or tracks your document text contents, file names, or personal media inputs.
